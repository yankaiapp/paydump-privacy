PayDump Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

PayDump does not require account registration and does not collect personally identifiable information.

Information Collection

All income settings, timer records, and history data are stored locally on your device. PayDump does not upload, sync, or transmit this data to external servers.

Network and AI Services

PayDump does not use internet connectivity, cloud services, analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or AI APIs.

Data Storage

Your data remains on your device. If you delete the app, locally stored data may also be deleted.
